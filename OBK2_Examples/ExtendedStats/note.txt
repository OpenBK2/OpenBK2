Optional mechanical-unit settings

Copy ExtendedStats.xdb into the same data/mod directory as the artillery's
MechUnitRPGStats.xdb, then add this reference inside <MechUnitRPGStats>:

    <ExtendedStats href="ExtendedStats.xdb" />

These settings belong to the artillery (or other mechanical unit), not its truck.
An absent reference keeps the original behavior. Each field can be omitted to use
its default: ForwardTowed=false, VerticalOffsetAngleRad=0.0, SpecialSightsArray empty.

ForwardTowed=true makes the artillery face in the truck's direction during hookup
and towing. Author LTowingPoint at the front of the artillery model and re-export
its stats so TowPoint contains that front position. The code uses this authored
point unchanged; it does not mirror a rear locator. The truck still attaches at
its rear. ForwardTowed=false expects the original rear artillery towing point.
In exported TowPoint coordinates, positive y is in front and negative y is behind.
Enabling ForwardTowed while retaining a negative y prevents the points meeting,
so attachment and crew boarding cannot start. Re-export the stats after moving
LTowingPoint, or set the intended front TowPoint explicitly in the unit stats.

VerticalOffsetAngleRad is added to the computed ballistic launch elevation,
in radians. Positive raises the shot; negative lowers it. The example adds 0.2
radians (about 11.46 degrees). Shell speed adjusts to reach the original target
with the new elevation and unchanged gravity. The arc and flight time change;
weapon range, normal dispersion and the intended explosion point are preserved.
Gun model rotation is unchanged.
The setting applies to the unit's ballistic shells (including cannon, howitzer
and grenade arcs); instant shots, guided missiles, bombs and other non-ballistic
trajectories retain their existing behavior. The existing interpolation between
launch and impact terrain heights is retained. Offsets that would launch downward
or backward cannot reach the target; those retain the original trajectory.

SpecialSightsArray adds any number of sight sectors to the normal view. See
SpecialSights.xdb for a forward hull sector and a narrower turret sector. To try
that example, reference SpecialSights.xdb instead of ExtendedStats.xdb, or copy
its SpecialSightsArray into your existing ExtendedStats record.

Each entry has AngleStartRad, AngleEndRad and SightRange (all default to 0.0),
and PlatformName (empty by default). Angles are relative to the selected facing:
zero points forward and increasing angles follow the game's positive rotation.
Negative angles and intervals crossing zero are supported. Equal endpoints
disable the entry; a span of at least a complete turn (2*pi radians) is a circle.
Intervals narrower than the engine's angle resolution are ignored.

An empty PlatformName uses hull facing. Otherwise it must exactly match the
RotatePoint of a platform in the unit's platforms array. Replace "turret" in the
example with your unit's actual RotatePoint. Platform zero follows the hull;
other platforms follow their simulation turret's horizontal rotation as well as
the hull. Unknown names or unavailable turrets disable that entry.

SightRange uses the same units as the normal Sight stat. Sight bonuses and
penalties apply first, then AIConsts.WarFog.MaxRadius limits the effective range.
Non-positive ranges and non-finite values are ignored. Coverage uses the existing
fog tile and angle resolution, terrain obstruction, and camouflage rules. All
sectors originate at the unit's normal sight position. Overlaps count only once;
they never reduce the original view. Units hidden inside transports or buildings
do not gain visibility from these entries.

Suggested in-game checks with your authored model:
- Hook up, drive straight, turn in both directions, stop and deploy the artillery.
  The front towing point should remain attached to the truck's rear, also on hills.
- Repeat with a rear-towed unit without ExtendedStats to check legacy behavior.
- Fire at targets at several ranges with positive, negative and zero offsets;
  check the changed arc and speed, and that impacts remain near the selected target.
- Save/load while towing and while aiming, and check the same sequence in replay.
- Turn the hull, then aim the turret while stationary, and check that each extra
  sector follows the selected facing while normal circular sight remains.
- Move, destroy or transfer the observer, including with overlapping sectors;
  check that its former coverage closes when no other unit sees that area.
